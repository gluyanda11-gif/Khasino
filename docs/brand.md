# Khasino identity

Approved mark: logo 19, a rounded stepped card outline and central solid diamond. Primary treatment: cream on ink, or ink on cream.

Current palette: ink #0A1F17, cream #F6EFDC, gold #E0B13C, felt #0F5132. Table lighting transitions from #117A45 in the centre to #0A3D26 at the edge, with faint baize noise. Suit red #C8312B appears on playing cards.

Every player uses the same geometric woven-style card back, including logo 19. The pattern is original decorative geometry rather than a claim of an authentic traditional textile. The table has a visible dark rail and a fine gold edge.

Enabled table actions have cream fills and dark text; Chow has gold. Disabled actions use faded outlines. Small in-game count labels are 12–13px. Preserve card pips, indices, soft shadows and restrained selection lift. Home copy is direct, and its preview shows a loose card, a 5+3 build and a hand holding eights.
